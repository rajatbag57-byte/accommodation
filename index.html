About ASTHI AHOB UTSAV

Date - Sunday, November 22, 2026 (Bengali Date - 6th Agrahayan, 1433)
Venue - Batanagar Newland adjacent field.

With the blessings of His Holiness Parampujyapada Sri Sri Acharyadev and the divine inspiration of His Holiness Parampujoniyo Abindada. we, the good friends of Alipore Subdistrict, have undertaken to organize the 'Asti-Ahab' festival on the occasion of the auspicious 139th birth anniversary of the most beloved Sri Sri Thakur Anukulchandra on Sunday, November 22nd, at the field adjacent to New Land, Batanagar.
Let us gather together on this great festival of Amritvarshi, be illuminated by the infinite blessings of the Supreme Being, and become successful in everything we do. Vande Purushottamam.
Program Following - Nahvat and Vedamangaliki. Ushakirtan. Morning prayer, chanting of names, and reading books. Then the next meal. Devotional music, bhajans, kirtans and Ishta discussions are held periodically throughout the day. Distribution of fruits in local hospitals. All-day medical services. Providing legal advice. Providing educational advice to students by experienced educators. vandara at afternoon. Evening preyar and thakur books read. Then: cultural variety show. Announcement ending of the festival.

A peaceful accommodation experience for Utsav participants.
ASTHI AHOB is a spiritual Utsav associated with Sri Sri Thakur Anukulchandra. This accommodation portal has been created to help devotees, families and guests find suitable accommodation during the Utsav.

Our purpose is to provide a simple and organized system for accommodation booking, room availability and stay management.
